Keyboard shortcuts

  • Alt+Shift+M: talk (push to talk). Press again to stop listening.
  • Alt+Shift+R: repeat the last reply.
  • Type or say help for every command. A few: "add it in medium", "what do people say about it", "save it for later", "it's a gift", "my orders", "where is my order", "how much did I spend this week", "return the hoodie", "order it again".
  • Escape: stop the app voice or listening. With a payment on hold, the same press also cancels it. In the 10 seconds after your PIN, one press of Escape undoes the payment.

About this demo

The shop, its sellers and the money are fake (PayPal sandbox). Reviews are fictional demo reviews written with Gemini for this prototype, not from real customers; their counts and averages come from that demo data. A few products carry made-up dark patterns (a subscription, a pre-ticked add-on, high shipping, "only 2 left") so you can hear the warnings. Trusted-contact approval links are shown in the conversation instead of emailed.

Privacy

The Talk button uses your browser's speech recognition. In Chrome, that sends your voice to Google to turn it into text. Typing works just as well and sends nothing to Google. We keep no recordings, no photos and no transcripts.

Sightline is a PayPal AI Hackathon prototype. Payments use the PayPal sandbox.
