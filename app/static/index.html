    How Sightline uses PayPal
    1. You link PayPal once, on PayPal's own login page. We never see your password; PayPal saves your wallet for Sightline.
    2. When you check out, PayPal puts the money on hold. Nothing is charged yet.
    3. The Readback reads PayPal's own numbers: items, shipping, tax and total.
    4. You say confirm, then type your PIN. You have 10 seconds to undo.
    5. Only then does PayPal take the payment. Cancel or undo, and PayPal releases the hold.
    6. Returns are refunded by PayPal, after the same Readback, confirm and PIN.

    Trusted contact (optional)

    Orders over the approval amount need this person's approval before you can pay. If Sightline suspects a scam (a gift card, or words like "the IRS told me to"), it asks them too, whatever the amount; you can turn that off below. Leave the name empty for no contact. Changing this needs your current PIN.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    • Alt+Shift+M: talk (push to talk). Press again to stop listening.
    • Alt+Shift+R: repeat the last reply.
    • Type or say help for every command. A few: "add it in medium", "what do people say about it", "compare those two", "save it for later", "tell me if it gets cheaper", "it's a gift", "pay later", "my orders", "where is my order", "what did I buy last month", "check my package", "it arrived broken", "return the hoodie", "order it again".
    • Escape: stop the app voice or listening. With a payment on hold, the same press also cancels it. In the 10 seconds after your PIN, one press of Escape undoes the payment.

    About this demo

    The shop, its sellers and the money are fake (PayPal sandbox). Reviews are fictional demo reviews written with Gemini for this prototype, not from real customers; their counts and averages come from that demo data. A few products carry made-up dark patterns (a subscription, a pre-ticked add-on, high shipping, "only 2 left") so you can hear the warnings. Trusted-contact approval links are shown in the conversation instead of emailed. Gift cards and scam-pressure words need the trusted contact, unless the shopper turns that off in Settings. Pay in 4 is read aloud, but this prototype pays in full; Pay Later would be chosen on PayPal's own page.

    Privacy

    The Talk button uses your browser's speech recognition. In Chrome, that sends your voice to Google to turn it into text. Typing works just as well and sends nothing to Google. A package photo is sent to Google Gemini to compare it with your order. We keep no recordings, no photos and no transcripts.
